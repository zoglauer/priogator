Installing Priogator
====================

1. Drag "Priogator" onto the "Applications" folder in this window.

2. Eject this disk (right-click it in the Finder sidebar → Eject).

3. Open Priogator from your Applications folder (or with Spotlight: ⌘ Space,
   type "Priogator").


The first start: "Priogator Not Opened"
---------------------------------------

Priogator is free software and not registered with Apple, so the first time
macOS shows a warning ("Apple could not verify Priogator is free of
malware…") and refuses to open it. You have to allow it once:

1. Click "Done" in the warning.

   Do NOT click "Move to Trash". That deletes the app. (If it happened:
   take it out of the Trash, or drag it from the disk image into
   Applications again.)

2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.

3. Scroll down to the Security section. Next to "Priogator was blocked to
   protect your Mac" click "Open Anyway".

4. Confirm with "Open Anyway" again and enter your password (or use
   Touch ID).

From then on Priogator opens normally.

On older macOS versions (before macOS 15) you can instead right-click
Priogator in Applications, choose "Open", and click "Open" in the dialog.


Alternative: with the Terminal
------------------------------

Open the Terminal app and run:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Priogator.app

Then open Priogator normally.
